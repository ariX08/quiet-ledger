About

A private ledger for focused time. Nothing more.

Why this exists

Most tools that track work try to motivate you. They add streaks, points, leaderboards, or social pressure. Quiet Ledger does the opposite. It simply records how much focused time you actually spent and lets you look at it later without judgment.

The goal is clearer self-knowledge, not more activity.

What it records

  • Date of the session
  • Duration in minutes
  • An optional short note (max 500 characters)

Privacy

All data is stored only in this browser using localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party scripts.

If you clear your browser data or switch devices, the sessions will be gone. This is intentional. The tool is meant to stay private and local.

What it deliberately does not do

  • No streaks or daily goals
  • No social features or sharing
  • No charts that try to “inspire” you
  • No accounts or cloud sync
  • No notifications

Accessibility

The interface is built with semantic HTML, visible focus states, proper labels, and respect for the reduced-motion preference. It is designed to work well with keyboard and screen readers.

Data stays in this browser.
